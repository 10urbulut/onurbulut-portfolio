TunerOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

TunerOS ("the app") is an instrument tuner, chord library, metronome and ear trainer made by Onur Bulut ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the sound from your microphone is analysed on your device and never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. There is no account. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data for those ads, as described below. Apart from that, the app sends us only usage statistics and error reports under a random installation number, which you can turn off.

What the app accesses, and why

The app asks for permissions only when it needs them. You can change them at any time in your device's settings.

Permission Why Where the data goes
Microphone To hear your instrument and show how far each note is from the target pitch. Used only while the tuner is listening. Analysed in real time on your device. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
Tracking (iPhone and iPad) Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show ads based on your interests. If you don't, it is not shared. Google AdMob, as described under “Advertising”.
Advertising ID (Android) Android gives apps that show ads access to the device's advertising ID (AAID). You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings. Google AdMob, as described under “Advertising”.

Pitch detection, reference tones, the metronome and the ear trainer all run entirely on your device. The app contains no audio recordings; every sound it plays is generated on the spot.

Advertising

The free version of TunerOS shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner, occasional full-screen ads between actions, and an ad when you open the app. TunerOS Pro removes all of them: while Pro is active, the app does not request any ads.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) appears the first time you open the app and asks whether ads may be personalised. On iPhone and iPad, the app then shows Apple's tracking prompt.
  • What Google may collect: your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking; the advertising ID on Android), an approximate location derived from your IP address, information about the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic and performance data about the ads. Google uses this data to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and, if you agreed, to personalise ads, under its own policies. Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • If you don't consent: you still see ads, but they are not personalised and not based on your interests or activity in other apps.
  • Changing your choices: where the consent form applies, use Settings › Pro & ads › Privacy options in the app at any time. On iPhone and iPad, go to the Settings app › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, open your phone's ad settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.

We do not receive any of the data Google collects for ads in a form that identifies you; we only see totals in our AdMob account, such as how many ads were shown.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No audio, and nothing you play, ever leaves your device.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not sell your data. Apart from the advertising described above, no data is shared with third parties.

Usage statistics and error reports

To see which parts of the app are used, where people get stuck and what goes wrong, the app sends us a record of what happens in it, under a random installation number: for example that the app was opened, that microphone access was allowed or denied, which instrument and tuning was selected, that a string was tuned, that the metronome was started, that the TunerOS Pro screen was shown or a purchase was completed, or that a setting was changed.

  • Error reports: when an error happens in the app, it sends the type of error, the first 300 characters of the error message and one line showing where in the app's code it happened. These are technical messages from the app and its libraries and are not meant to contain personal data.
  • Sent with each event: a random installation number that the app creates the first time it is opened, the app version, the operating system and its major version, the app language, the day the app was first opened and the number of days since then, and a second random number for each session. The installation number lets us follow how one installation of the app is used over time. It is not your device's identifier, an advertising identifier or an account, it does not tell us who you are, and it is gone when you delete the app.
  • Never sent: audio, anything you type (such as the names of saved metronome settings), your name, email address or contacts, your location, or an advertising or device identifier.
  • Our server runs on Cloudflare and notes the country a request comes from. Your IP address is not stored.
  • The statistics are not linked to your name, account or any other identity, are not used for advertising, are not combined with data from other apps or companies, and are not sold to or shared with anyone. They are deleted after 13 months.
  • You can turn this off at any time in Settings › App › Share usage statistics. When you turn it off nothing more is sent, the installation number is erased from your device, and the app asks our server to delete everything recorded under that number.

Purchases

TunerOS Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we never receive your payment details. The app only asks the store whether your subscription is active, and remembers that answer on your device.

Your settings

Your instrument and tuning, reference pitch and other settings, and saved metronome settings are stored only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app. If your device backs up app data (for example iCloud or Google backup), that backup is handled by Apple or Google under your own account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the free version shows ads, users who do not consent to personalised ads are shown ads that are not personalised.
