TunerOS Support

TunerOS tunes 21 instruments, from guitar, bass and ukulele to bağlama, oud and cello, plus a chromatic mode for everything else. It also has a chord library, a metronome and an ear trainer, all in one app.

  • iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later
  • Android 7.0 or later
  • No account; audio never leaves your device

Frequently asked questions

The tuner doesn't react when I play

TunerOS needs microphone access to hear your instrument. If you declined the prompt, the Tuner screen says “Microphone access needed”. Tap Open settings there, or turn it on yourself:

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings app › TunerOS › Microphone.
  • Android: Settings › Apps › TunerOS › Permissions › Microphone, then allow it while using the app.

If the screen says “Microphone could not start”, another app (a call, a recorder or a video app) may be using the microphone. Close it and try again.

For a steady reading, tune somewhere quiet, play one string at a time and let it ring. Hold the phone close to the instrument, especially for quiet instruments or low bass strings. If the needle never settles, try a wider Sensitivity in Settings.

How do I change the reference pitch (A4) and other tuning settings?

Open the Settings tab. Under Tuning:

  • Reference pitch (A4): from 415 to 466 Hz in 1 Hz steps. The default is 440 Hz. Use it for baroque pitch, an orchestra tuned to 442 Hz, or to match a piano that sits a little sharp or flat.
  • Sensitivity: how close a note must be to count as in tune, from ±1 to ±20 cents (default ±5). Lower is stricter, higher is easier to lock on.
  • Note names: letter names or Do Re Mi.

Under Instrument you can pick the instrument and tuning (including alternate tunings such as Drop D or DADGAD), turn Automatic string detection on or off, and switch on Left-handed mode. You can also change the instrument by tapping its name on the Tuner screen.

How do I remove the ads?

TunerOS is free with ads. TunerOS Pro is a yearly subscription that removes all of them: banner ads and full-screen ads. Everything else in the app works the same with or without Pro.

Go to Settings › Pro & ads › TunerOS Pro. The price is shown there before you subscribe and may vary by country.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings › Pro & ads › TunerOS Pro and tap Restore purchase at the bottom of the screen.

Use the same Apple Account or Google account you subscribed with. Restoring needs a connection to the App Store or Google Play. If the store has no active subscription for that account, TunerOS says “No active subscription found”. When Pro is active, the Settings row reads “TunerOS Pro is active”.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not inside the app. While Pro is active, tapping TunerOS Pro is active in Settings opens your store's subscription page. Or go there directly:

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › TunerOS › Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play › your profile picture › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › TunerOS › Cancel subscription.

To avoid the next renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I change my privacy choices for ads?

Ads in the free version come from Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears the first time you open the app. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Pro & ads › Privacy options. This row only appears where the consent form applies.

On iPhone and iPad, TunerOS also asks whether it may track you for personalised ads. You can change that in the Settings app › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Privacy › Ads or Google › Ads, depending on the phone.

If you don't allow personalised ads, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests. More detail is in the Privacy Policy.

What does “Share usage statistics” do?

It sends us counts of which parts of the app are used, plus error reports, under a random installation number. No audio and nothing that identifies you is sent. You can turn it off in Settings › App › Share usage statistics; when you do, the app also asks our server to delete what it recorded for your installation. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15.0 or later, and Android phones and tablets with Android 7.0 or later.

The tuner works offline. An internet connection is only needed for ads, purchases and usage statistics.
