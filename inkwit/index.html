In English

Every question comes with three clues, from hard to easy. The earlier you get it, the more points you score. Merak the octopus never scolds you for a wrong answer; it just looks surprised and shows you a fact card.

  • English and Turkish
  • No account
  • Plays offline
  • No ads during questions
