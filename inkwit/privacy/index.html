Inkwit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This page explains what the Inkwit quiz game ("the App"), published by Onur Bulut ("we"), processes, why, and where. We kept it short; if anything is unclear, write to us.

1. In short

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos or contacts.
  • We have no server. Your points, streak, jokers and settings stay on your phone. No game data reaches us.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. To show an ad, Google processes some device information (section 4). Personalised ads are shown only if you allow them.
  • Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not use analytics tools, crash-tracking tools or social sign-in.

2. What stays on your phone

As you play, the following is written to your phone's local storage: total points and round results, report-card statistics, daily streak, remaining jokers, whether you bought "No Ads", your chosen language and difficulty, and your sound, music, vibration, reduce-motion and reminder-time settings.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the App deletes all of it. Your phone's own backup (iCloud backup or Android auto backup) may back this data up to your Apple or Google account; that backup is yours and we cannot access it.

3. Daily reminder (notifications)

The reminder only works if you turn it on; your phone asks for notification permission when you do. The notification is scheduled and shown by your phone's own clock. No server, push token or push service is involved. You can turn it off any time in Settings › Daily reminder.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads keep Inkwit free. There are no banner ads. There are two kinds:

  • Rewarded video: only when you tap "watch" (e.g. to refill a joker, get an extra life or double your round points).
  • End-of-round ad: the first 3 rounds are ad-free; after that, only when a round ends and at least 2 minutes apart. No ads during a question, while the timer runs, or when you open the App from a notification. Buying "No Ads" removes end-of-round ads.

To show and count ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software may process:

  • IP address (to estimate approximate country/city),
  • device and advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID and App Set ID; on iOS the identifier for vendors; the iOS advertising identifier IDFA only if you allow it),
  • technical information such as device model, operating system and language,
  • ad interactions (impressions, taps, whether a video was watched) and the ads software's own diagnostics.

This information goes directly to Google over an encrypted connection. We do not access it; in the AdMob dashboard we only see totals (impressions, earnings). How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Google may process this data on servers outside your country.

5. Permissions and your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other places where required): on first launch, before any ad loads, Google's consent form is shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Manage ad privacy.
  • Tracking permission on iPhone (ATT): the App asks so that ads can be more relevant to you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not read and everything in the game still works. You can change this in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads.

Saying no to ads never locks any part of the game; a rewarded video may just be unavailable at times.

6. Purchases

"No Ads" and joker packs are sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google takes the payment and handles it under their own privacy policies. Your phone only receives which product you bought and that the transaction was approved; we do not send this to any server. On a new phone you can get "No Ads" back with Restore purchases in the Shop. Jokers you have used are not restored.

7. If you email us

Settings › Report a wrong question, or the report link on a question, opens a ready draft in your email app. The draft contains the question number, the game language and your phone's operating system and version. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and you can edit the draft first.

We use your email and email address only to reply and fix the question. We do not share it and delete it once the matter is done.

8. Children

Inkwit is designed for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you notice this has happened, please write to us.

9. Legal basis (GDPR and Turkish KVKK)

The data controller is Onur Bulut. Game data kept on your phone never reaches us. Processing by the ads software is based on your consent where required (consent form and ATT), and otherwise on the legitimate interest of offering a free service and preventing fraud. Email correspondence is processed to answer your request.

Under the GDPR and Article 11 of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you have the right to know whether your data is processed, to request correction or deletion, to object, and to withdraw consent. Write to us for any request; we reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Board). Requests about ad data can also be sent directly to Google.

10. Changes

If the App starts processing new data (for example, if a server is added), we will update this page beforehand and change the date above.
