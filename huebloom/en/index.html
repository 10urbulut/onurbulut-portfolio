Huebloom Support

Huebloom is a calm yet captivating match-3 puzzle where you swap shimmering gems and juicy fruit. No lives, no waiting, no pay-to-win.

  • iPhone, iOS 15 or later
  • Android 7.0 or later
  • No lives, no purchases
  • Ads only if you choose

Frequently asked questions

What kind of game is Huebloom?

A puzzle where you match three or more identical pieces to complete level goals. There are 500+ levels, with a boss level every five. Alongside the main journey you can play the Daily Puzzle (the same board for everyone), Zen (unlimited moves), 60 Seconds and the Workshop, where you design your own levels. Pick between jewels under the night glow or a sunlit fruit orchard.

Where is my progress stored? How do I reset it?

Levels, stars, best scores, boosters, Workshop levels and settings are stored only on your device. No account is needed and the game plays offline.

Because of this, progress does not move between devices and is lost if you uninstall the app. To start over, go to Settings › Reset all progress; this cannot be undone.

Are there ads?

Only if you want them. There are no banner or interstitial ads. When you want a booster you can choose to watch a short video, at most 3 times a day. Ads are requested as non-personalized.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and other regions where required) a consent message is shown before the first ad. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options. Declining does not lock any part of the game; only the rewarded video option may be unavailable.

How do I turn off usage data?

An anonymous usage counter that does not know who you are helps us improve the game (for example, which level players get stuck on). Turning off Settings › Usage data stops sending and asks the server to delete every record of that install. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I share a Workshop level?

After designing a level in the Workshop, tap Copy code; a short code starting with HB1- is written to your clipboard. Send it to a friend, who can copy it and tap Paste in the Workshop to open and play your level. The clipboard is only used when you tap copy or paste.

What does “Watch the bot” do?

When you lose a level, Watch the bot on the result screen shows how the same board can be solved. You can change its speed and take back control at any time. A round played by the bot never counts toward your progress, stars or best scores. You can also long-press a special piece to preview what it hits, and the Guide explains every combo.

Are there lives or purchases?

No. There is no life counter and no waiting; if you lose, try again right away. There are no in-app purchases either. Every level gives you 3 undos, and after three tries on the same level you get a free hammer.

Which devices are supported?
  • iPhone, iOS 15 or later
  • Android phones, Android 7.0 or later

There is no separate iPad version. The game is currently available in Turkish only.
