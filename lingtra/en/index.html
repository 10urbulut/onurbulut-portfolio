Lingtra Support

A translation editor with translation memory and a termbase that keeps your formatting. For translation students and freelance translators.

Open a Word document, translate it sentence by sentence, and export it with the same layout. Bold, italics, footnotes, tables and images stay where they were. Every sentence you confirm goes into your translation memory, your terms are found even with Turkish suffixes, and missing tags or mismatched numbers are flagged as you type.

  • Mac and iPad, App Store
  • French → Turkish
  • No account, no server
  • No ads, no tracking

Frequently asked questions

Which devices can I use?

Mac and iPad, both from the App Store. When you are signed in with the same Apple Account, one Lingtra Pro subscription works on both.

There is no iPhone version.

Which files can I open?

Word documents (.docx). Glossaries as CSV/TSV (“Save as CSV” from Excel), translation memories as TMX.

Which language pairs are supported?

French → Turkish only, for now. More pairs will follow in updates; tell us which one you need.

Where is my work stored?

Only on your device. No account, no cloud, no Lingtra server. Your original Word file is never modified; the translation is saved as a separate “_TR.docx” file.

How do I back up my work or move it between Mac and iPad?

The app interface is in Turkish. On the home screen, open the backup menu and choose Yedek al (Create backup). Lingtra writes your projects, translation memory and terms into a single .lingtra file. Keep that file wherever you like: iCloud Drive, another cloud folder or a USB drive.

On the other device (Mac or iPad), choose Yedekten yükle (Restore from backup) in the same menu. Restoring replaces the local data on that device; it does not merge. For security, your API keys are not included in backups; enter them again on the new device.

How do AI suggestions work?

They are optional. If you enter your own API key for Google Gemini, OpenAI or DeepL in Settings, you can get a suggestion for the sentence you are translating. The key is stored on your device, in the system keychain.

When you ask for a suggestion, only that sentence and your matching glossary terms are sent directly from your device to the provider you chose; Lingtra has no server and never sees the text. AI usage is not included in the subscription; the provider's own pricing applies, if any. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I take my translation memory to another tool?

Yes. Export it as TMX from the Bellek (Memory) tab; any translation tool that reads TMX can open it. You can also import TMX files from other tools.

What is included for free?

Every feature, for 1 project and up to 500 terms. An auto-renewing yearly Lingtra Pro subscription removes both limits.

How do I cancel my subscription?
  • Mac: App Store › your account › Account Settings › Subscriptions › Lingtra Pro
  • iPad: Settings › Apple Account (your name) › Subscriptions › Lingtra Pro

You keep Pro until the end of the current period. Your projects are never deleted, and you can still open and export them.

I subscribed but don't see Pro.

Tap Satın alımları geri yükle (Restore Purchases) on the purchase screen and make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Something from my document is missing in the translation.

Known limits: text boxes, SmartArt and Word comments are not translated yet; fields such as a table of contents are preserved, but the text inside them is not translated. If anything else is lost, write to us, ideally with a sample document.
