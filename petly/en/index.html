Petly Support

Petly keeps your pets' health and care records in one place: vaccines, medication, vet visits, weight, documents, expenses and reminders. Add as many pets as you like.

  • iPhone, iOS 17 or later
  • No limit on pets
  • No ads, no data selling

Frequently asked questions

What is free?

Your records, reminders, supplies list and data export are always free, with no limit on the number of pets. Automatic records from documents are limited to 3 documents a month on the free plan and unlimited with Premium. Family and sitter sharing and the vet PDF summary are Premium features.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings › Petly Premium › Restore purchases. You must be signed in with the Apple Account you bought Premium with.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not the app: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Petly › Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next renewal. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. The lifetime plan is a one-time payment.

How do automatic records from documents work?

You take a photo of a receipt, prescription or vaccine card; the text is read on your phone by its built-in text recognition and turned into a record suggestion. The document is not sent to any third party for reading. You can review and correct suggestions before saving.

How do I share a pet with someone?

Sharing is a Premium feature. Create an invite code under Sharing on the pet's page and send it. The person you invite sees only that pet's records. You can remove access from the same place at any time.

How do I add to the supplies list with Siri?

After opening the app at least once, say "Add food to Petly list" or "Petly is out of food". For an item that is not on the list, say "Add something to Petly list" and Siri will ask what to add.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings › Account › Delete my account. Deleting it permanently removes pets you solely own, their records and files. You can export your data first in Settings › Export my data.

If you cannot sign in, write to the address below from your account email and we will delete it for you.

Does Petly give medical advice?

No. Petly is a record-keeping tool; it does not diagnose or recommend treatment or doses. Consult your veterinarian for any health decision.
