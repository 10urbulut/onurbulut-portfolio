Petly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Data controller

Onur Bulut, Türkiye. Contact: help@onurbulut.dev

Data we process

  • Account: your email address, name and optional phone number.
  • Pet records: your pets' identity details, health and care records, reminders, supplies and expenses.
  • Files: photos and documents you upload.
  • Device: a device token used to deliver notifications.
  • Messages to us: what you send from the "Contact us" screen, with your app version and device type; your email address if you ask for a reply.
  • Usage statistics: which screens and features are used; app version, operating system, language and country. Kept under a random install number that is not linked to your account; your IP address, your records and anything you type are not stored. Turning it off in Settings deletes this install's counts; counts are kept for at most 13 months.

Purposes

Storing your records and syncing them across your devices, sending reminders, enabling sharing with people you choose, and managing your account.

Automatic records from documents

The text in the document you select is read on your device, using its built-in text recognition, and turned into record suggestions; the document is not sent to any third party for this. The document itself is uploaded to your archive like your other files.

Transfers

Your records and files are stored on Cloudflare and your sign-in on Google Firebase; these providers' servers are located outside Türkiye. Your data is never shared or sold for advertising or marketing.

Sharing

People you invite to a pet can see that pet's records. If you enable the public emergency card, only the fields you select are visible to anyone with the link.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in Settings > Account. Deleting it permanently removes pets you solely own, their records and files. You can always export your data for free in Settings > Export my data.

Your rights

To exercise your data protection rights, write to help@onurbulut.dev.
