Petly Terms of Use

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The service

Petly is a record-keeping tool that helps you track your pets' health and care and get reminders.

Not medical advice

Petly does not diagnose, or recommend treatment or medication doses. Summaries and information extracted from documents are only an organised form of what you entered or what the document says. Consult your veterinarian for any health decision. In an emergency, go directly to a veterinary clinic.

Accuracy of records

You keep the records and are responsible for their accuracy. Review information extracted from documents before saving it.

Account and sharing

You are responsible for the security of your account. You decide what invited people and the public card can see.

Paid features

Your records, reminders and data export are always free, with no limit on the number of pets. Premium features are unlocked with a monthly or yearly subscription. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period; manage or cancel them in your store account settings.

Changes

These terms may be updated; we will announce significant changes in the app.
